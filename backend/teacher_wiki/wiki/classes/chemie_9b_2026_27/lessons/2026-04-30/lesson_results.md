## What was covered
- Balanced simple equations by changing coefficients only.
- Used atom counts to check the law of conservation of mass.
- Reviewed the difference between coefficients and subscripts.

- The atom-count table helped students justify each correction.

- Some mixed up the meaning of coefficient and subscript in the first round.
