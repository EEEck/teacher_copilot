# Chemistry Grade 9 NTG - representations and models

- Name whether a task is working at substance, particle, or symbolic level.
- Ask students to translate between levels and explain what is preserved or
  lost in the translation.
- Use model revision to make changed reasoning visible; a labelled diagram on
  its own is not evidence of model-based explanation.
