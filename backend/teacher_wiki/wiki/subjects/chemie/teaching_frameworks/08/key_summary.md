# Chemistry Grade 8 NTG - key summary

## Effective principles

- Treat Grade 8 as prior learning for Chemistry 9 NTG, not as assumed mastery.
- Reconnect observable substance properties to particle models and simple
  chemical reaction descriptions before extending symbolic work.
- Re-establish safety, evidence recording, and the difference between an
  observation and an explanation when a new practical sequence begins.
