review-only candidates; ledger folding and admission gates suppress noise;
distinct occasions reinforce inferred evidence; Sweep performs one bounded
semantic consolidation judgment; and the teacher approves any durable write.

favor of canonical `course_state.md` and `timeline.md`. MemV4 is the active
contract and evaluation home; detailed MemV3 implementation notes remain under
