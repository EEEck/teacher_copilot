# Chemistry differentiation guidance

## Shared core
- Keep the same chemical question, phenomenon/context, evidence and central objective.
- Below supports observation, representation and explanation without revealing the answer.
- At preserves the grade-level task and expected reasoning.
- Above adds transfer, quantitative reasoning, an alternative explanation or evaluation.

## Scaffolding
- Use a concrete representation, vocabulary support or structured observation prompt.
- Fade embedded supports across the task sequence; do not scaffold every item equally.
- Keep grouping flexible and revisable from the formative check.

## Student-facing language
- Do not print “Differenzierung”, “Scaffold”, “Tier” or teacher-only rationale.
- Use neutral headings such as “Beobachte”, “Ordne”, “Erkläre” and “Prüfe deine Idee”.
