# Lesson Plan - Introduction to Redox

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-21

## Learning goals
- Define oxidation and reduction as electron transfer.
- Identify the oxidized and reduced species in a simple example.
- Separate redox language from the old "oxygen only" idea.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Recall one oxidation-number example from last lesson.
- **Input** (15 min): Introduce oxidation and reduction with electron movement.
- **Practice** (15 min): Read simple examples and label oxidized/reduced species.
- **Close** (10 min): Summarize how redox differs from plain charge checking.

## Warmup
- Quick review of oxidation numbers and one common mistake.

## Practice
- Label oxidation, reduction, oxidizing agent, and reducing agent in two short examples.
- Compare the electron-transfer language with the oxygen-based language.

## Homework
- Complete a short redox vocabulary check.
- Prepare one example where oxidation does not involve oxygen.

## Teacher notes
- Use concrete examples and do not drift into metals too early.

## Addresses open loops
- Oxidizing agent versus reducing agent
- Electron-transfer language

## Addresses misconceptions
- Oxidation is not limited to reactions with oxygen.
- Redox is about electron movement, not just charge labels.
