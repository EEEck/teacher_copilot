# Lesson Plan - Balancing Chemical Equations

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-04-30

## Learning goals
- Balance simple chemical equations by changing coefficients only.
- Explain the law of conservation of mass in student language.
- Separate coefficients from subscripts without hesitation.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Review one skeleton equation from the last lesson.
- **Input** (15 min): Model balancing a simple equation with atom counts on the board.
- **Practice** (15 min): Paired balancing of short reaction examples.
- **Close** (10 min): Share the final coefficients and justify them with atom counts.

## Warmup
- Quick recall: what can change when balancing, and what cannot?

## Practice
- Balance four simple equations.
- Mark the atom counts before and after each change.

## Homework
- Complete the balancing worksheet.
- Rewrite one example with a short explanation of the coefficient choice.

## Teacher notes
- Keep the class on coefficients only; pause immediately if anyone changes a subscript.

## Addresses open loops
- Word-to-symbol conversion
- Atom counts on both sides of the arrow

## Addresses misconceptions
- Do not change subscripts to balance.
- Balancing preserves the identity of the substance.
