# LehrplanPLUS Physik 8 NTG

## Summary
Grade 8 NTG is the first physics year: circuits, optics and mechanics, each introduced from an observable phenomenon and a simple model, with an NTG profile block on solar cells and other electricity sources.

## Section: ph8_lb1 — Elektrischer Strom
Students build and describe simple circuits, distinguish the conditions for a closed working circuit, and use current, voltage and resistance qualitatively to explain what a circuit does. Measurement and safe low-voltage practice are established here.

## Section: ph8_lb2 — Optik
Students explain reflection and refraction with a straight-line light model, construct simple ray diagrams, and use the model to predict what an optical arrangement will do — including where the model's assumptions stop holding.

## Section: ph8_lb3 — Mechanik
Two blocks: dynamics of two-dimensional motion, and forces and their effects. Students represent motion and forces graphically, distinguish a force from its effect, and connect everyday situations to a force description.

## Section: ph8_lb4 — Profilbereich am NTG
The NTG profile block. Investigations on solar cells and other sources of electricity (mandatory), plus deepening of process competencies on selected further content. This block is what distinguishes the NTG course from the common Gymnasium plan.
