# Misconceptions

## 2026-05-29
- Students treated chloride, oxide, and phosphate as special cases instead of ordinary ions.
- Some still mixed up ion charge with oxidation number.

## 2026-05-25
- A few students described the metal displacement examples as memorized facts instead of electron transfer.
- Some did not yet connect metals activity to the redox vocabulary.

## 2026-05-21
- Several students still thought oxidation always required oxygen.
- Reduction was sometimes treated as "taking away oxygen" instead of gaining electrons.

## 2026-05-14
- Oxidation number was confused with ionic charge.
- Some students tried to infer redox from charge only.

## 2026-05-07
- Students still wanted to change subscripts when balancing equations.
- A few forgot that coefficients preserve the molecule identity.

## 2026-04-30
- Several students balanced by trial and error without first counting atoms.
- Skeleton equations were still written too loosely.

## 2026-04-24
- Students confused formula order with word order.
- Some needed reminders that the skeleton equation comes before balancing.
