# Chemistry Grade 11 NTG - key summary

## Effective principles

- Grade 11 is context-driven: the chemistry is carried by food and pharmaceutical
  applications rather than by a new theoretical concept.
- Anchor each context in structure–property reasoning students already own, then
  extend to the functional groups the context demands.
- Applied contexts invite everyday claims; keep the evidence standard explicit
  and separate what the chemistry supports from what marketing asserts.

## Scope orientation

- Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten — methods and models at Oberstufe entry.
- Lebensmittelchemie — food chemistry as an applied context.
- Pharmazie — pharmaceutical chemistry as an applied context.
