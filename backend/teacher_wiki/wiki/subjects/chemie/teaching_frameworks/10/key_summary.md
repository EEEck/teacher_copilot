# Chemistry Grade 10 NTG - key summary

## Effective principles

- Grade 10 extends the donor–acceptor idea from Grade 9 across three reaction
  families; treat it as one organising concept, not three separate topics.
- Reversibility and equilibrium are the new demand. Make the "both directions at
  once" picture explicit before quantitative treatment.
- Keep the link between symbolic notation, particle-level story, and observable
  substance behaviour visible in every reaction type.

## Scope orientation

- Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten — methods, models, and evidence.
- Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept bei Protonenübergängen — acids/bases and reversibility.
- Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept bei Elektronenübergängen — redox reactions.
- Nukleophil-Elektrophil-Reaktionen — reversibility in organic reaction types.
