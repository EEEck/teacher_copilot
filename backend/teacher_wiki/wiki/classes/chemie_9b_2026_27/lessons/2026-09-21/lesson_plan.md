# Lesson Plan — Introduction to Redox

> Duration: 45 min
> Date: 2026-09-21

## Learning goals
- Define oxidation and reduction in terms of electron transfer
- Identify oxidizing and reducing agents in simple examples

## Lesson flow
- **Einstieg** (10 min): Recall valence electrons from last week
- **Input** (15 min): Board examples of redox without and with oxygen
- **Practice** (15 min): Pair exercise — classify species
- **Reflection** (5 min): One-minute paper — "What is still unclear?"

## Warmup
Ask: "What happens to electrons when sodium reacts with chlorine?"

## Practice
- Pair task: worksheet 2 tasks 1–2
- Class debrief on board

## Homework
Worksheet 2, tasks 1–4
