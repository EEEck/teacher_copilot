# Teaching Patterns

> Class: chemie_9b_2026_27
> Class + subject teaching style: how THIS class learns and which approaches work or fail for it.

## Class Learning Profile
- Students thrive on concrete examples and need visual supports before grasping abstract symbolic rules.
- Regular scaffolding is essential to differentiate ion charge from oxidation number.

## What Worked Well  
- Concrete examples effectively aided understanding of balancing and redox concepts.  
- Peer checking minimized errors in distinguishing coefficients from subscripts during balancing activities.  
- Real-world applications, like metal displacement, clarified the concept of electron transfer.  

## What Didn't Work Well  
- Some students overly simplified complex examples, missing the underlying principles connected to oxidation states.  
- Transitioning from distinguishing ion charge to oxidation number was sometimes unclear without explicit contrasts and comparisons.
