## What was covered
- Assigned oxidation numbers in simple compounds and ions.
- Compared oxidation number with ionic charge.
- Practiced common rules for oxygen, hydrogen, chlorine, and metals.

## Student participation
- The class followed the rule list better once it stayed on the board.
- Alex Weber and Lena Hoffmann were quick to apply the rules.
- Kathy Braun and Joonho Kim still mixed up charge and oxidation number at first.

## What went well
- The direct rule list kept the pace under control.
- Students could now see why oxidation numbers matter before redox.

## What didn't go well

- A few needed the examples repeated once more before they felt settled.
