# Lesson Plan - Redox Reactions with Metals

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-25

## Learning goals
- Use metal displacement reactions to identify redox change.
- Link metal activity to electron transfer.
- Name the oxidized and reduced species in the examples.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Review one redox example from last lesson.
- **Input** (15 min): Show metal displacement as a concrete redox case.
- **Practice** (15 min): Analyze two or three metal reactions in pairs.
- **Close** (10 min): Summarize what changed and why.

## Warmup
- Recall oxidation, reduction, and the agent labels.

## Practice
- Decide which metal is oxidized and which species is reduced.
- Write one sentence explaining the electron transfer.

## Homework
- Finish the metal reaction explanation sheet.
- Prepare one example for the next lesson where the ion part matters more.

## Teacher notes
- This lesson should set up the shift toward anions and oxidation-state review.

## Addresses open loops
- Oxidized and reduced species in metal displacement
- Real examples of redox without the oxygen story

## Addresses misconceptions
- Metal reactions are not just memorized patterns.
- Redox language still depends on electron transfer.
