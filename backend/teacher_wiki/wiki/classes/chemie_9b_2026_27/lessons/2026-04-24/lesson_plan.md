# Lesson Plan - Reaction Writing Basics

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-04-24

## Learning goals
- Write skeleton equations from simple word prompts.
- Use the correct element symbols and basic compound formulas.
- Check atom counts before any attempt to balance.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Quick symbol check for common metals and non-metals.
- **Input** (15 min): Model turning word prompts into skeleton equations.
- **Practice** (15 min): Paired conversion of three short reaction prompts.
- **Close** (10 min): Compare answers and identify formula or symbol errors.

## Warmup
- Board recall: Mg, O, Cl, H, Na, Ca, Fe.

## Practice
- Convert three word reactions into skeleton equations.
- Circle the atoms that must stay constant across the arrow.

## Homework
- Finish the reaction-writing worksheet.
- Bring one self-corrected skeleton equation to the next lesson.

## Teacher notes
- Keep the pace tight and do not move to balancing until skeleton equations are solid.

## Addresses open loops
- Word-to-symbol conversion
- Common element symbols

## Addresses misconceptions
- A formula subscript is part of the substance, not a balancing tool.
- The skeleton equation comes before balancing.
