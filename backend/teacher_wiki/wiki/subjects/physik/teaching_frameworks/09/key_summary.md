# Physics Grade 9 NTG - key summary

## Effective principles

- Start from an observable phenomenon or a measurement students can argue about,
  then introduce the model that explains it. Physics vocabulary should land on a
  concrete referent students have already seen.
- Make the modelling step explicit: name what the model predicts, where it
  breaks, and which quantity is actually being measured.
- Keep the mathematical treatment in service of the physics. Ask students to say
  in words what an equation claims before rearranging it.
- Pair each practical with a short prediction and a reconciliation step —
  predict, measure, explain the gap.
- Safety, apparatus feasibility, and realistic timing belong in the plan, not in
  the teacher's head; low-voltage and prepared kits keep the focus on reasoning.
- Preserve one shared physical question across differentiated routes so a class
  discussion is still possible after group work.

## Scope orientation

- Energie als Erhaltungsgröße — mechanical and electrical energy, conservation as the organising idea.
- Atome — a first particle account of matter.
- Wärmelehre — particle model, thermal energy transport, and climate relevance.

The NTG profile content is “Profilbereich am NTG (climate, plus deepening of process competencies)”. The remaining Lernbereiche are common to all Gymnasium branches; the profile block is what makes this an NTG course and is mandatory in part.
