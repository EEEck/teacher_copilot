# LehrplanPLUS Physik 11 NTG

## Summary
Grade 11 NTG covers circular motion and wave physics, then gives substantial space to student-led independent work; the NTG profile block adds numerical methods and photovoltaics.

## Section: ph11_lb1 — Kreisbewegung
Circular motion, described quantitatively and connected to the force required to sustain it.

## Section: ph11_lb2 — Schwingungen und Wellen
Oscillations and waves as a shared description of periodic phenomena, with model transfer between mechanical and other wave contexts.

## Section: ph11_lb3 — Eigenverantwortliche Arbeit an physikalischen Themen
Student-led work on astronomical world views, an introduction to special relativity, and energy supply. The emphasis is on students planning, sourcing and presenting physics independently.

## Section: ph11_lb4 — Profilbereich am NTG
The NTG profile block: the method of small steps (numerical iteration), photovoltaics in applications, an out-of-class activity, and deepening of process competencies.
