# LehrplanPLUS Physik 10 NTG

## Summary
Grade 10 NTG extends fields and conservation laws, makes motion modelling quantitative, and introduces nuclear physics; the NTG profile block treats semiconductor devices.

## Section: ph10_lb1 — Elektromagnetismus
Electric and magnetic fields and electromagnetic induction, developed as field models that explain observable effects.

## Section: ph10_lb2 — Impulserhaltung in der Mechanik
Momentum as a second conserved quantity alongside energy, applied to collisions and interactions.

## Section: ph10_lb3 — Bewegungen und ihre Modellierung in der Physik
Quantitative modelling of motion: students set up, apply and judge the limits of a motion model rather than only substituting into formulas.

## Section: ph10_lb4 — Kernphysik
Nuclear processes, radiation and their applications, including risk and evidence-based judgement.

## Section: ph10_lb5 — Profilbereich am NTG
The NTG profile block: semiconductor devices in experiments (mandatory), plus deepening of process competencies on selected further content such as sports physics, flight physics or nanotechnology.
