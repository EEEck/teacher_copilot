# Lesson Plan - Anions and Oxidation State Review

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-29

## Learning goals
- Review common anions and their charges.
- Separate ion charge from oxidation number.
- Connect chloride, oxide, and phosphate back to the redox sequence.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Recall one metal redox example from the last lesson.
- **Input** (15 min): Review simple anions and oxidation-state language.
- **Practice** (15 min): Compare chloride, oxide, and phosphate in short examples.
- **Close** (10 min): Summarize how the ion examples fit the redox unit.

## Warmup
- One short recall question on oxidized versus reduced species.

## Practice
- Assign charges and oxidation numbers in a few simple ions.
- Compare the ions to the earlier redox and metal examples.

## Homework
- Finish the anion comparison sheet.
- Review the oxidation-number rules before the next unit.

## Teacher notes
- Keep the distinction between charge and oxidation number visible throughout the lesson.

## Addresses open loops
- Anion review
- Charge versus oxidation number

## Addresses misconceptions
- Common ions are not exceptions to the chemistry logic.
- Oxygen is common, but redox language is broader than oxygen reactions.
