# LehrplanPLUS Chemie 9 NTG

## Summary
Grade 9 NTG develops atomic structure and the shortened periodic table, ion formation, molecular structure and models, with planned investigations and explicit competency work.

## Section: c9_atombau — Atombau und gekürztes Periodensystem
Students use experimental findings and periodic-table data to describe energy levels, characterize atoms, determine valence electrons and infer ion charges, while comparing model representations and their limits.

## Section: c9_molekuele — Moleküle und Struktur-Eigenschafts-Beziehungen
Students use model representations to relate molecular structure, bonding and properties, and explain the scope and limits of chemical models.

## Section: c9_methoden — Chemische Erkenntnisgewinnung
Students derive questions from simple everyday phenomena, plan hypothesis-led experiments, collect and interpret data and communicate evidence in correct chemical language.
