# Lesson Results - 2026-05-25 - Redox Reactions with Metals

> Class: chemie_9b_2026_27 | Subject: chemie
> Raw: [2026-05-25-redox-reactions-with-metals](../../../raw/classes/chemie_9b_2026_27/2026-05-25-redox-reactions-with-metals.md)

## What was covered
- Explored metal displacement as a redox example.
- Linked metal activity to electron transfer language.
- Identified the oxidized and reduced species in the examples.

## Student participation
- Most students were engaged because the examples felt concrete.
- Sven Fischer, Matt Keller, and Alex Weber were quick to reason through the cases.
- Kathy Braun and Joonho Kim were slower and needed more prompting.

## What went well
- Real examples helped students move from vocabulary into reasoning.
- The class could usually name the oxidized and reduced species after discussion.

## What didn't go well
- A few students still memorized the pattern instead of explaining it.
- Some needed extra help connecting the examples back to the redox vocabulary.

## Student observations
- Sven Fischer: Strong on reasoning through the displacement examples.
- Matt Keller: Good at conservation checks and the metal examples.
- Alex Weber: Clear in explanation and quick with the agent labels.
- Kathy Braun: Needed support to keep the language precise.
- Joonho Kim: Better when the example stayed concrete.
- Mira Lange: Strong in discussion.

- Name the oxidized and reduced species in that paragraph.
