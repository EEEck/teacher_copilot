# Chemistry Grade 9 NTG - differentiation

- Retain one chemical question and core evidence task for the class.
- Vary access through particle drawings, vocabulary support, structured data,
  paired roles, and prompts that fade after the first successful attempt.
- Make teacher diagnostics explicit in the teacher section, never in the
  student-facing material.
