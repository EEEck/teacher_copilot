# LehrplanPLUS Chemie 8 NTG

## Summary
Prior-learning baseline for NTG Chemistry: how chemists think and work, substances and properties, chemical reactions, particle models, safety, simple calculations and evidence-based observation.

## Section: c8_foundations — Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten
The Grade 8 baseline introduces observations, hypotheses, experiments, documentation, safe handling and the separation of substance-level observations from particle-level explanations.

## Section: c8_reactions — Stoffe und chemische Reaktionen
Students characterize observable substance and energy changes, interpret reactions with particle models and use simple equations and quantity relationships.
