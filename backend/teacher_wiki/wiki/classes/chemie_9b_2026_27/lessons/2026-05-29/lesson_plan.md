# Lesson Plan — Next lesson

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-29

## Learning goals
- Understand anions with a focus on phosphate and its oxidation states.
- Recognize simple anions like Cl and O.

## Lesson flow
- **Opening** (5 min): Quick diagnostic warmup on oxidation numbers, especially related to phosphorus.

- **Main teaching** (25 min): 
  - Discuss oxidation states of phosphorus, using PO₄²⁻ and Na₃P as examples. 
  - Introduce and explain simple anions like Cl⁻ and O²⁻  
  - Explain how these relate to their respective oxidation states.

- **Practice** (10 min): Pair work to diagram oxidation states and identify oxidizing/reducing agents using the discussed anions.

- **Close** (5 min): Reflection on key points and misconceptions regarding oxidation.
