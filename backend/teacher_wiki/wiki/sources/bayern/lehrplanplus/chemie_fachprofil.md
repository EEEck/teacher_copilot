# LehrplanPLUS Gymnasium Chemistry Fachprofil

## Summary
The Fachprofil describes Chemistry as a subject about substances, properties and transformations, and organizes learning through Sachkompetenz, Erkenntnisgewinnungskompetenz, Kommunikationskompetenz and Bewertungskompetenz.

## Section: competency_domains — Kompetenzbereiche
Use the four competency domains to describe the relevant student work, without forcing every domain into every lesson.

## Section: representation — Stoff-Teilchen-Symbol
Connect observations at the substance level with particle-level models and symbolic chemical language; make model limits explicit.
