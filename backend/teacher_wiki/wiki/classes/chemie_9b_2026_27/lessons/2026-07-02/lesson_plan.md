# Lesson Plan — Next lesson

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-29

## Learning goals
- Understand anions with a focus on phosphorus (PO₄²⁻, Na₃P) and their oxidation states.
- Recognize simple anions such as Cl⁻ and O²⁻ and connect to their respective oxidation states.

## Lesson flow
- **Opening** (5 min): Quick diagnostic on oxidation numbers, particularly related to phosphorus.

- **Main teaching** (25 min):  
  - Discuss oxidation states of phosphorus using PO₄²⁻ and Na₃P as examples.  
  - Introduce and explain simple anions like Cl⁻ and O²⁻, emphasizing how these relate to oxidation states.

- **Practice** (10 min): Pair work to diagram oxidation states of phosphorus and simple anions, identifying oxidizing/reducing agents via discussed examples.

- **Close** (5 min): Reflection on key points and misconceptions about oxidation states and anions.

## Warmup
- 5-minute diagnostic on oxidation numbers focusing on phosphorus.

## Practice tasks
- Pair exercises on oxidation states for P, Cl, and O anions.

## Homework
- Review metals around chromium and first-row transition metals.  
- Work on practice problems related to Cl⁻, O²⁻, and examples of phosphorus anions.

## Teacher notes
- Address misconceptions about oxidation and provide concrete examples of how oxidation states function in anions.
