# Lesson Plan - Conservation of Mass and Coefficients

> Duration: 45 min | Target date: 2026-05-07

## Learning goals
- Explain conservation of mass using atom counts.
- Use coefficients to balance equations without changing formulas.
- Compare two corrected equations and justify the final coefficients.

## Lesson flow
- **Warmup** (5 min): Spot the mistake in one deliberately unbalanced example.
- **Input** (15 min): Revisit the atom-count method and the conservation-of-mass rule.
- **Practice** (15 min): Pair work on corrected equations and atom tables.
- **Close** (10 min): Whole-class check of the hardest examples.

## Warmup
- Identify what stayed constant across the arrow in a balanced equation.

## Practice
- Complete an atom-count table for two equations.
- Rewrite the same equation with a different incorrect coefficient choice and fix it.

## Homework
- Balance the worksheet problems and underline the atoms that stayed fixed.

## Teacher notes
- The class is ready for a stronger connection between balancing and later redox work.

## Addresses open loops
- Atom-count table on the board
- Coefficients versus subscripts

## Addresses misconceptions
- Conservation of mass is checked with atom counts, not with formula changes.
- Coefficients do the balancing; formulas stay the same.
