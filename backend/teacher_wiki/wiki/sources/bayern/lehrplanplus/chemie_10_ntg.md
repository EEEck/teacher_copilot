# LehrplanPLUS Chemie 10 NTG

## Summary
Grade 10 NTG carries the donor–acceptor concept across three reaction families and introduces reversibility as the new organising demand.

## Section: c10_lb1 — Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten
Methods, models and evidence standards: planning investigations, distinguishing observation from explanation, and judging the reach of a chemical model.

## Section: c10_lb2 — Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept bei Protonenübergängen
Acid–base chemistry as proton transfer, with reversibility and equilibrium treated explicitly rather than as a one-directional arrow.

## Section: c10_lb3 — Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept bei Elektronenübergängen: Redoxreaktionen
Redox as electron transfer, extending the Grade 9 treatment and connecting oxidation numbers to reversible processes.

## Section: c10_lb4 — Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept bei Nukleophil-Elektrophil-Reaktionen
Nucleophile–electrophile reactions, bringing the same donor–acceptor idea into organic reaction types.
