# Chemie — Subject Guide

> Compact subject-wide guidance and trusted-source TOC. Detailed guidance and source bodies are linked and read on demand.

## Core planning principles
- Connect substance-level observations, particle models and symbolic equations.
- Make experiments evidence-generating, with realistic safety and timing.
- Separate chemical reasoning from memorized rules.

## Common lesson patterns
- Board introduction → guided examples → pair practice → worksheet.
- Safety reminder before any lab or demonstration.

## Typical misconceptions (Gymnasium Chemie)
- Oxidation number is not the same as ionic charge.
- Oxidation is not limited to reactions with oxygen.

## Differentiation principles
- Keep the same chemical question, context and core evidence across learners.
- Add representations, vocabulary and structured prompts; fade supports.
- Use flexible, revisable grouping based on formative evidence.
