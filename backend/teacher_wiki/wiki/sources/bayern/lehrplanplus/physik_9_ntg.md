# LehrplanPLUS Physik 9 NTG

## Summary
Grade 9 NTG makes energy the organising idea, adds a first particle account of matter, and develops thermal physics with explicit climate relevance; the NTG profile block treats climate.

## Section: ph9_lb1 — Energie als Erhaltungsgröße
Mechanical energy (kinetic, potential, elastic) and electrical energy. Students describe processes in technology and nature as energy conversions, work quantitatively where appropriate, and use conservation as the constraint that makes a prediction possible.

## Section: ph9_lb2 — Atome
A first model of matter as composed of atoms, used to explain observable behaviour rather than as a fact to memorise.

## Section: ph9_lb3 — Wärmelehre
The particle model, then thermal energy transport and its influence on climate. Students separate temperature from thermal energy and use the particle picture to explain conduction, convection and radiation.

## Section: ph9_lb4 — Profilbereich am NTG
The NTG profile block: climate (mandatory), plus deepening of process competencies on selected further content. Students argue from data about a controversially discussed topic.
