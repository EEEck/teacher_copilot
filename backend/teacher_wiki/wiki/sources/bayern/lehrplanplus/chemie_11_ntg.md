# LehrplanPLUS Chemie 11 NTG

## Summary
Grade 11 NTG is context-driven: the chemistry is carried by food and pharmaceutical applications rather than by a new theoretical concept.

## Section: c11_lb1 — Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten
Methods and models at Oberstufe entry, with an explicit evidence standard for applied claims.

## Section: c11_lb2 — Lebensmittelchemie
Food chemistry: structure–property reasoning applied to nutrients and food components, extended to the functional groups the context demands.

## Section: c11_lb3 — Pharmazie
Pharmaceutical chemistry through medicines in the digestive system and analgesics, connecting molecular structure to physiological effect.
