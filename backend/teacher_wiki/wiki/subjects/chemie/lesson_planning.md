# Chemistry lesson-planning guidance

## Before drafting
- Determine the lesson's Grade 9 NTG scope and unit position.
- Read the relevant LehrplanPLUS section before claiming curriculum alignment.
- Name one enduring chemical idea and observable student actions.
- Identify a concrete prerequisite and the likely point of difficulty.

## Chemistry-specific design
- Connect substance-level observations, particle-level explanations and symbolic equations.
- Make experiments generate evidence before the explanation is settled.
- Use realistic 45-minute timing, safe materials and observable look-fors.
- End with an evidence-based formative check that tests the central reasoning.

## Teacher-facing quality
- State competency domains only when the lesson uses them.
- Record what students do, why the difficulty occurs and the teacher's next move.
- Cite source IDs and sections actually read; never invent official citations.
